# Ghar Stock — Play Store listing

## App name
Ghar Stock: Pantry & Medicine

## Short description (max 80 characters)
Grocery lists, pantry stock, expiry tracking and family medicine doses. Offline.

## Full description
Ghar Stock helps your family keep track of what is at home, what to buy, and which medicines to take, all in one simple app.

**Shopping lists**
- Make separate lists like Kiryana, Sabzi or Medical Store
- Add items with quantity, unit, category and price
- Tick items as you buy them, then move them to your home stock in one tap

**Home stock (pantry)**
- Keep track of groceries in the fridge, freezer, kitchen or store room
- Search, filter by category or place, and sort by expiry date
- Red for expired items, orange for items expiring within 3 days
- Add a low item to a shopping list straight from the stock screen

**Expiry tracking**
- Home shows items and medicines that are expired or expire within 3 days

**Family medicines**
- Add medicines for each family member
- Flexible schedules: fixed times every day, every few hours, chosen days of the week, or a course for a number of days
- Mark each dose as Taken or Skip from the Home screen
- Pill count goes down automatically when you mark a dose as taken
- Low pill warning in the app when only 3 days of doses are left
- Full dose history for every medicine

**Home dashboard**
- Today's medicines with quick Taken / Skip
- Items expiring soon
- How many items are in stock and how many are still to buy

**Monthly report**
- See how much you spent on groceries each month, broken down by category

**Backup & restore**
- Export all your data to a file and restore it on a new phone

**Private by design**
- Works 100% offline. No internet permission, no account, no ads, no tracking.
- All data stays on your phone.
